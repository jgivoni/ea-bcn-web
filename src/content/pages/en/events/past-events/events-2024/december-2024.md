# December: A lovely finish to the year ✨

In December, we **finished our first introductory fellowship**, co-hosted a **talk on the power of effective giving** and played some **highly original boardgames**. We thank everyone who got involved for helping us finish off this year on a high note, and we are very much looking forward to seeing what 2025 brings!

### A talk on the power of effective giving

EA Barcelona and [ESAlogika](https://esalogika.com/) organized an event on **the power of effective donations for creating meaningful change**. We also enjoyed **swapping pre-loved items** and **networking with like-minded changemakers**, and hope participants left inspired to make a real difference in the lives of others, this Christmas season and beyond!

### We concluded our first Introductory Fellowship!

For five weeks, our group of thoughtful, curious participants came together to explore some of the **most pressing challenges facing humanity today**, and the **strategies we can use to address them**. We'd like to thank everyone who participated for your unique perspectives and for helping us make this first iteration a success!

(Sad you missed out? Remember you can always [join a virtual program](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/virtual-programs/introductory-program) from anywhere. And we plan to run more fellowships in 2025 too!)
