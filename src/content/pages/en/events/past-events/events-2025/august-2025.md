### Community Spotlight 🔦

This month, we're celebrating [Martin Lange](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martin-lange-9b69a7251/), a familiar face to many in our community! Now back in Germany, Martin recently won the prestigious **Best Computer Science Graduate** 24/25 award at [Karlsruhe](https://www.linkedin.com/company/begabtenstiftung-informatik-karlsruhe/).

Instead of keeping the prize, Martin chose to turn his achievement directly into impact. He donated the full €3,000 prize money to [Effektiv Spenden's](https://www.linkedin.com/company/effektiv-spenden-org/) Animal Welfare Fund, a fund dedicated to supporting the **most effective animal advocacy organizations.**

In his own words, Martin highlighted that this cause is often **one of the most neglected.** He explained, _"The majority of farm animals suffer in factory farming, where conditions are particularly terrible. You don't have to be an animal lover to count this among the biggest problems of our time."_ By funding organizations like [Sinergia Animal](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sinergia-animal/), his donation helps drive corporate campaigns for cage-free eggs and **reduce suffering on a massive scale.**

**Congratulations**, Martin, on your incredible achievement and for providing such an inspiring example of turning personal success into meaningful impact! 💚
