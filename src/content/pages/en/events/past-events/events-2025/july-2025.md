# 🌳 July: Big Ideas & Bigger Connections

This month took us from quantum theory to Ugandan classrooms, from shared desks in Barcelona cafés to deep chats in Zaragoza. July was all about community! Connecting across regions, sharing stories, and building something bigger together. Let’s take a look at what happened 👇

Community Spotlight 🔦

This month, we’re spotlighting a true milestone: the first EA Spain Summer Retreat, held in a quiet villa outside Zaragoza with 22 attendees from across the country.

The weekend brought together EAs working on everything from AI safety and mental health to community building and effective giving. We shared meals, hosted strategy sessions, got real about movement-building (and yes, played our fair share of board games.) [Check out the recap on Linkedin here.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/effective-altruism-barcelona_this-past-weekend-we-made-a-little-bit-of-activity-7356322737801379842-7WUW?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAACQu-cIBWZ5cSD9IsInNq7gLG-7s7mDjWBI)

Some ideas that came out of the retreat:

-   A mentorship-style collaboration between [EA Barcelona](https://www.linkedin.com/company/effective-altruism-barcelona/?viewAsMember=true) & [EA Madrid](https://www.linkedin.com/company/effective-altruism-madrid/posts/?feedView=all)

-   The launch of a national EA book club this fall featuring [Pablo Melchor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pmelchor/)’s new book [Altruismo Racional](https://pablomelchor.com/libro/)

-   New partnerships forming across cause areas like AI x Philosophy and Mental Health x Community

Big thanks to everyone who made it happen, especially the organizers and volunteers who made the weekend feel both productive and personal. If this sounds like your kind of gathering, stay tuned… this was just the beginning!

👩🏽‍🏫 Sofia Martínez Gálvez: From Physics to Global Impact

We were thrilled to welcome [Sofía Martínez Gálvez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/smartinezgalvez/) earlier this month for a powerful talk on her journey from quantum physics to founding [Learning Alliance](https://www.linkedin.com/company/learning-alliance-africa/posts/?feedView=all), a nonprofit improving education in Uganda. With over 20 people in the room — many new faces! — Sofia’s story raised reflection on risk, impact, and building with humility.

🎥 You can now watch the talk on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIoj2bWSGUo)!
