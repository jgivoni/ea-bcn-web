# 🎉🎄November & December: An Impactful Finish to the Year

EA Barcelona is excited to wrap up an incredible 2025! Over the past two months, we’ve run an **_Altruismo Racional_ book club**, hosted a **Probably Good career workshop** on finding high-impact roles, the **EA Connect watch party**, and closed the year with a social gathering to reflect on **wins, share ongoing projects, and look ahead**. Together, these activities helped us deepen our thinking, support each other’s goals, and strengthen the connections that make our community thrive.

### Community Spotlight 🔦 Karoteno’s December Fundraising Campaign!

You may remember [Karoteno](https://karoteno.org/), the new giving platform founded by EA Barcelona community member [Guillem Lajara](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillem-lr/), with the ambitious goal of normalising effective giving in Spain. This December, Karoteno took a big step forward with its [first matching fundraising campaign](https://karoteno.org/diciembre-2025), supported by the community and amplified by a _zambomba solidaria_ put on by **Pablo Rosado** in his hometown of **Jerez**.

Thanks to the generosity of many contributors, **over €6,000** was raised and redirected toward highly effective charities in **global health, animal welfare**, and **safeguarding the future of humanity**. We’d like to extend a huge congratulations to Guillem on a very successful first campaign - and thank you to everyone involved in making this happen!

### “Altruismo Racional” Book Club 📚

Throughout November-December, EA Barcelona hosted a book club on [Altruismo Racional](https://www.altruismoracional.com/p/libro) by Pablo Melchor, with a small, engaged group and **thoughtful, high-quality discussions** about how to do more to help others. **Shout-out** to our star facilitators, and thank you to everyone who participated with such curiosity and generosity!

### Probably Good Workshop 🔨

In mid-November, Rocky from [Probably Good](https://probablygood.org/) led 12 of us through the [SELF framework](https://probablygood.org/core-concepts/self-framework/) (Significance, Efficacy, Leverage, and Personal Fit). Using concrete examples and targeted questions, we explored how to evaluate potential roles through these four lenses - all with the aim of moving closer to a high-impact career.

Thanks to everyone who joined us, and we’re excited to plan the next workshop soon: How to (actually) get a job that makes a difference 🔍

### EA Connect Viewing 📺

On the first weekend of December, we hosted an **in-person viewing** of the **EA Connect conference**. 12 participants enjoyed engaging with **high-quality talks** and **real-life conversations** which were just as valuable. Topics covered AI, animals and careers (and even popcorn 🍿).

It was great to see new connections forming - a big **thank you** to everyone who came along and helped make the event run smoothly!

We wrapped up the year with our final coworking session at **Sopa**. It was a productive and enjoyable close to the year at our **favourite Poblenou spot**.
