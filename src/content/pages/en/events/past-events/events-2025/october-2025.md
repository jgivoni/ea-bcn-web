# 🎲 October: From Rational Altruism to Boardgame Chaos

October was packed - from a full house at the **_Altruismo Racional_ book launch** with Pablo Melchor, to an **AI Safety Barcelona talk tackling the risks of advanced AI**. Our coworking session dove into **task automation and career reflections**, while our social night proved that **good company matters more than boardgame skills** ;)

### 📚 Pablo + Pablo: Altruismo Racional

We had the pleasure of listening to **Pablo Melchor** at **Llibrería +Bernat** for the launch of his first book, [Altruismo Racional](https://www.altruismoracional.com/p/libro), in a fascinating conversation guided by **Pablo Rosado**. ### 💻 How to make AI safe?

[AI Safety Barcelona](https://www.aisafetybcn.org/) hosted a two-speaker technical talk at **Universitat de Barcelona**. Speakers **Alejandro Tlaie** (Pour Demain) and **Pablo Moreno** (Xanadu AI & VRAIN) shared insights on **evaluating and mitigating risks from advanced AI systems**, as well as answering some intriguing questions from the audience.

A big **thank you** to the AI Safety BCN team for organizing and to everyone who came and participated!

### Test Kitchen Confessions with the Alt. Protein Club

We hosted a new **Alt. Protein Club** event in collaboration with [Naked Innovations](https://www.nakedinnovations.eu/), creating space for honest conversations about the realities of working in alternative proteins.

We heard candid stories from **Camilla Ariano**, who shared how aligning her scientific background with her values led her into plant-based food, and **Joost Lindeman**, co-founder of [_Be Better My Friend_](https://bebettermyfriend.com/), who spoke openly about the challenges of building a startup and the importance of adapting when things don’t go as planned.
