Community Spotlight 🔦

This month, we wanted to celebrate the incredible work of our long-time member and co-founder of EA Barcelona, [Pablo Rosado.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pabloarosado/)

As many of you know, Pablo has a Youtube channel called [Altrufísica](https://www.youtube.com/@altrufisica) where he combines science outreach with altruism to generate even more positive impact outside of his already impactful job at [Our World In Data](https://ourworldindata.org/).

We’re bringing Pablo to your attention because he just finished creating his most challenging video yet, and it's an absolute must-watch. It tackles one of the most common and controversial questions posed by animal welfare sceptics: "Do farm animals really live that badly?"

🎥 Watch it [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aj33EzC0P1Y)! 

This isn't an easy watch, but it's an important one. Pablo goes deep into the topic with the kind of nuance and storytelling that makes you think critically. It’s a great example of using clear communication and visuals to make a real impact on a difficult topic.

If this video makes you think differently, here are three easy ways to help it reach more people:

-   Give it a 👍 on YouTube.

-   Leave a quick comment (it really helps the algorithm!).

-   Share it with one person who you think should see it.

Amazing work, Pablo! Thanks for pushing this vital conversation forward 👏

🤖 A Deep Dive into Generative AI at 42 Barcelona

[AI Safety Barcelona](https://www.aisafetybcn.org/) hosted a special and insightful talk at the innovative 42 Barcelona campus. The session explored Generative AI, covering how state-of-the-art models are built and the complex risks they pose.

The event even featured live jailbreaking demos, giving attendees a hands-on look at the technology's vulnerabilities and strengths. A big thank you to the AI Safety BCN team for organizing and to everyone who came and participated!
