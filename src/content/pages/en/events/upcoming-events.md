# Our upcoming events in Barcelona.

Events will be published here as they are confirmed. Make sure you subscribe to our [Luma calendar](https://lu.ma/calendar/cal-9tdD2nUvxmygpXa) too in order to receive notifications, and read our [code of conduct](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YoJtHXtnxY2tzt5TZHpIped4RDW7oDxKE9Veb-tHb4A/edit?usp=sharing) for all our events. Additionally, you can check out recaps of past events [here](/events/past-events).

Note that not all events published here are organized by EA Barcelona. Some come from other organizations and individuals inside or outside the EA movement. We promote these events because they are likely to be relevant to people interested in EA in general.
